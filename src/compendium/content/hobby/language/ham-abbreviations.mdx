## Short words for a slow medium

Ham jargon grew out of Morse code, where every character costs time. "Fine business" became **FB**, "good evening" became **GE**, and a handful of the shortcuts leaked into everyday speech on the air. You do not need to memorize them: read a few QSOs and they come quickly.

## Anatomy of a CQ call

<HamAbbreviations_CqCall />

**CQ** is the general call: "anyone who hears this, please answer". **DE** is French for "from" and works as "this is". **K** means "go ahead". Put together: CQ CQ CQ DE N0CALL N0CALL K. A contest call is "CQ TEST", and "CQ DX" asks for distant stations only.

## Everyday abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **73** | Best regards (the usual sign-off) |
| **88** | Love and kisses (among friends) |
| **ANT**, **RIG**, **PWR** | Antenna, radio (rig), power |
| **TX**, **RX** | Transmit, receive |
| **WX** | Weather |
| **HR**, **UR** | Here; your or you're |
| **ES** | And |
| **FB** | Fine business: excellent |
| **GM, GA, GE, GN** | Good morning, afternoon, evening, night |
| **HI** | Laughter (the sound of a chuckle in Morse) |
| **OM**, **YL**, **XYL** | Old man (any male ham), young lady (any female ham), wife |
| **OP** | Operator |
| **AGN**, **PSE** | Again, please |
| **TNX**, **TU** | Thanks, thank you |
| **VY** | Very |
| **CFM** | Confirm |
| **HPE**, **CUL** | Hope, see you later |
| **SIG** | Signal |
| **DX** | A distant station (usually another country) |
| **SWL** | Shortwave listener: someone who listens but does not transmit |
| **HT** | Handheld transceiver |

Terms you will hear on the air: **Elmer** (a mentor), **shack** (the room where the station is), **ragchew** (a long, relaxed conversation), **pileup** (many stations calling one at once), **hamfest** (a swap meet and gathering), **QRP** (low power).

## Procedural signals

On CW, prosigns such as AR, SK, BT, AS and KN are sent as one run-together character (K is an ordinary letter):

| Prosign | Meaning | Morse |
|---|---|---|
| **K** | Go ahead, anyone | − · − |
| **KN** | Go ahead, only the station I am working | − · − − · |
| **AR** | End of message | · − · − · |
| **SK** | End of contact | · · · − · − |
| **BT** | Pause or break in the text | − · · · − |
| **AS** | Wait | · − · · · |

<Callout kind="tip" title="Plain language on voice">Abbreviations belong mostly to CW and written logs. On voice, say "thank you" and "good morning", not "TNX" and "GM". It is clearer, and it is easier on newcomers. Do not talk in jargon at a new listener.</Callout>
