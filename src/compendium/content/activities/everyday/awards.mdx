## What it is

An amateur radio award is a certificate (or listing) that recognises a collection: contacts with a set of places, people or things. Awards give a reason to keep operating, point you at new bands and modes, and give the log a purpose. They are friendly competition against your own record.

## From contact to certificate

<Awards_Pipeline />

The idea is always the same: work a station, **confirm** the contact, and send your proof to the sponsor. The part that fails most often is the confirmation: both logs must agree on call sign, band, mode and a time within a small tolerance. See <Ref to="logging-and-qsl" />. Whether a given confirmation counts depends on the program; read the rules before you start.

## Well-known examples

| Award | You collect | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| **DXCC** | DX entities | Contacts with places counted as separate entities, beyond your own, with a first threshold and many levels and endorsements above it. |
| **WAS** (Worked All States) | US states | A confirmed contact with every state, in whatever band and mode the version of the award asks. |
| **WAC** (Worked All Continents) | the world's continents | A confirmed contact on each continent. A good first award for a modest station. |
| **IOTA** | island groups | Contacts with a list of island groups, each identified by a reference. See <Ref to="special-events" />. |
| **POTA and SOTA** | parks, summits | Activating or hunting entries on a list. See <Ref to="parks-on-the-air" /> and <Ref to="summits-on-the-air" />. |

Each of these has its own bands, modes, endorsements and application process. Counts, levels, dates and fees change; **check the program's current rules.** Many also have **endorsements** for a single band, a single mode, or low power.

## Getting started

- **Keep an accurate log** from your first contact. See <Ref to="logging-software" />.
- **Join Logbook of The World** (or the program's accepted system) early; it saves a great deal of waiting for cards.
- **Let the software track it.** Many loggers show which entity, state or continent is still needed, and flag it when it appears on a spotting network.
- **Start with a goal you can reach.** A first WAC, or the states closest to you, builds confidence.
- **Remember the QSL.** Contacts that never confirm do not count, however good they were.

<Callout kind="note" title="Awards are not the point">An award is a nudge, not a requirement. Plenty of operators never apply for one; the log and the chase are the hobby.</Callout>
