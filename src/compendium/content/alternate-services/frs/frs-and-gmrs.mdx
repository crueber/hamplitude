## One set of channels, two services

<Callout kind="note" title="FRS needs no license. GMRS does.">FRS is not amateur radio and needs no individual license (95.305). **GMRS** needs its own individual license, covered in <Ref to="gmrs-licensing" />. They are different services with different rules that happen to share spectrum.</Callout>

All 22 FRS channels are also allotted to GMRS on a shared basis (95.563). So an FRS hand-held and a GMRS radio set to the same channel hear each other: FRS units may be used to communicate with GMRS stations (95.531(c)), and a GMRS station may talk to FRS units (95.1731).

## Side by side

| | FRS | GMRS |
|---|---|---|
| Rulebook | Part 95, Subpart B | Part 95, Subpart E |
| Individual license | none | required |
| Who may operate | any eligible person | licensee and immediate family |
| Power on channels 15 to 22 | 2.0 W ERP | mobile, base and repeater up to 50 W transmitter output |
| Power on channels 1 to 7 | 2.0 W ERP | 5 W ERP |
| Power on channels 8 to 14 | 0.5 W ERP | hand-held only, 0.5 W ERP |
| Antenna | fixed to the radio | not required to be fixed on mobile and base stations |
| Repeaters | none: FRS is defined for hand-held radios | eight repeater pairs |
| Identification | no requirement in Subpart B | call sign at the end and every 15 minutes |
| Equipment | FCC-certified | FCC-certified |

Power, antenna and repeater facts are in <Ref to="gmrs-channels-and-power" /> and <Ref to="gmrs-repeaters" />.

## Which fits what

This part is reasoning from the table, not a rule. FRS suits people who want a radio with no paperwork for short-distance voice at a gathering or on a trail. The fixed antenna and low power keep it simple and keep range short. GMRS fits when you want more range: a licensed household can use higher power on the main channels, an external antenna on a mobile or base station, and repeaters. A GMRS radio can still talk to everyone else's FRS radios on the shared channels, so the two mix freely in a group, as long as everyone transmitting on GMRS holds a license.
