## The license-free radio service

The **Family Radio Service (FRS)** is "a short-distance two-way voice communication service, with limited data applications, between low power hand-held radios, for facilitating individual, family, group, recreational and business activities" (47 CFR 95.503). It is the service behind the small hand-held radios sold in pairs.

<Callout kind="note" title="FRS is not amateur radio, and it needs no individual license">FRS falls under Part 95, the rules for the **Personal Radio Services**, not Part 97. The FCC authorizes eligible people to operate Part 95 stations without an individual license, except where a service's own rules require one (95.305). Its radios must still be FCC-certified. **GMRS**, which shares FRS channels, does need its own license: see <Ref to="gmrs-licensing" />.</Callout>

## The 22 channels

FRS has **22 channels**, and every one of them is also allotted to GMRS on a shared basis (95.563). Consumer radios number them 1 to 22. They fall into three groups:

| Channels | Band | Power limit (ERP) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 7 | near 462 MHz | 2.0 W |
| 8 to 14 | near 467 MHz | 0.5 W |
| 15 to 22 | near 462 MHz | 2.0 W |

**ERP** is effective radiated power: what the antenna sends out, including its gain (95.567). The full frequency list and a map of how the channels sit in the two bands are in <Ref to="gmrs-channels-and-power" />.

## What it is for

The definition points at **short-distance** use: family outings, a group on a hike or at an event, a work crew on a site. The low power limits and fixed antenna keep range short, mostly line of sight (see <Ref to="gmrs-radios-and-range" />). Voice is the main use. FRS units may also send **location information** or a **brief text message** to a specific FRS or GMRS unit, as long as each data transmission lasts no more than one second and is sent no more often than once every 30 seconds (95.531, 95.587).

FRS units may be used to talk with GMRS stations, and a GMRS station may talk with FRS units (95.531(c), 95.1731). More on that in <Ref to="frs-and-gmrs" />, and the limits that stand in for a license in <Ref to="frs-rules-and-limits" />.
