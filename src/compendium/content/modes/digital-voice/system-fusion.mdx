## What it is

System Fusion is Yaesu's digital voice system. Its radio signal uses **C4FM**, a four-level form of frequency modulation, and it is designed to live alongside ordinary FM: Fusion radios and repeaters can switch between analog FM and digital automatically.

## Four levels, two bits each

A plain FSK signal jumps between two frequencies, so each symbol carries one bit. C4FM uses four, so each symbol carries **two bits**: twice the data at the same symbol rate, in the same narrow channel.

## Two flavours of digital voice

Fusion radios offer a choice of how to spend the available bits. Two of the digital settings, in Yaesu's terms:

| Setting | Idea |
|---|---|
| **V/D** (voice and data) | Voice shares the bits with data such as GPS position. |
| **Voice FR** (full rate) | Most of the bits carry voice, which suits good audio on a strong signal, with little room for data. |

## Features worth knowing

- **Automatic mode select (AMS)** lets the radio choose FM or digital to match what it hears, so you can use one memory for either.
- **Group monitor** shows which Fusion stations are nearby and active.
- **WIRES-X** links repeaters and hotspots into "rooms" over the internet. You pick a room from the radio, much like selecting a talkgroup. See <Ref to="internet-linking" />.
- A **hotspot** or home node gives you low-power access; see <Ref to="hotspots-and-gateways" />.

Other digital formats do not decode Fusion, and Fusion radios do not decode them. Check what a repeater runs before programming it.
