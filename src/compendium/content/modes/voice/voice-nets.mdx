## What it is

A voice net is a scheduled on-air meeting on a set frequency, usually run by a **net control station** (NCS) who keeps order so many people can share one channel. Nets are used for club announcements, training, weather reports and emergency practice. This article is about the procedure; see <Ref to="nets" /> for the many kinds of nets and <Ref to="net-control" /> for running one in an emergency.

## Two styles

| | Directed net | Open (informal) net |
|---|---|---|
| Who speaks | Only when net control says | Anyone, in turn, by courtesy |
| Good for | Big groups, emergency drills, traffic | Small groups, social round tables |
| Control | NCS calls every station | Light touch, sometimes a rotating host |

Most nets mix the two: a directed check-in followed by looser conversation.

## The shape of a net

<VoiceNets_Outline />

Net control typically calls the net to order and directs communications between stations checking in. Everyone else follows one rule: **unless you are reporting an emergency, transmit only when directed by net control.**

**Traffic** means formal messages passed between net stations; see <Ref to="radiograms" />.

## Joining well

- **Listen first.** Hear the net's name, the NCS and how check-ins work before you speak.
- **Check in once**, with your call sign only, when invited. If the NCS wants more (name, location), they will ask. Say "short time" if you must leave early.
- **Use phonetics** for your call sign and for any unusual word or name.
- **Wait to be recognised** before sending your message, then keep it brief.
- **Say "emergency"** at once if you have urgent or emergency traffic. NCS will stop the net and give you the channel.
- **No tuning up, no side chatter** on net frequency.
- **Check out** when you leave, so net control is not left waiting for you.

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Net control | The station running the net |
| Checking in | I am here and ready |
| Short time | I can only stay a short while |
| Go ahead | You may transmit now |
| Standing by | I am listening, waiting for my turn |
| Clear | I have finished and am leaving the channel |
