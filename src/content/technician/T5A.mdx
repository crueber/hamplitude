<Concept id="quantities" title="Voltage pushes, current flows, power is the rate" qs="T5A01 T5A02 T5A03 T5A05 T5A10">

A difference in **voltage** pushes electrons along. Their flow is **current**. **Power** is how fast electrical energy is used.

<ElectricUnits keys={['voltage', 'current', 'resistance', 'power']} />

<Key>Volts push. Amperes flow. Watts measure how fast energy is used.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">Watt-hours and ampere-hours measure an *amount* used over time. Power and current are *rates*.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="conductors" title="Free electrons make a conductor" qs="T5A07 T5A08">

In metals, outer electrons are loose and drift when voltage pushes them. In an insulator, every electron stays bound to its atom.

<Conductors />

<Key>Metals have many free electrons. Glass, plastic and air do not.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">Sea water and graphite look harmless, but both conduct. Glass is the insulator.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="ac-dc" title="AC reverses direction. Frequency counts the cycles." qs="T5A04 T5A06 T5A09 T5A11">

**DC** flows one way. **AC** alternates between positive and negative directions. Complete cycles per second is **frequency**, in **hertz (Hz)**.

<AcDc />

<Key>AC swings both ways, not just to zero. Hertz = cycles per second.</Key>

Resistance opposes current of every kind: DC, AC and RF.

</Concept>
