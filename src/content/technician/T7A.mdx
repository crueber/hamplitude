<Concept id="transceiver" title="A transceiver is a receiver and transmitter in one box" qs="T7A02 T7A07 T7A09 T7A10">

<TransceiverBlocks />

**Transceiver** = transmitter + receiver. **PTT** (push-to-talk) switches between them: grounding the PTT input means **transmit**.

Want more power? Add an **RF power amplifier** to the transceiver's output. Its mode switch (SSB or CW-FM) sets the amplifier for **proper operation in that mode**. It doesn't change your signal.

<Key>PTT grounded = transmit. More power = RF power amplifier.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="mixers" title="Oscillators make frequencies, mixers move them" qs="T7A03 T7A05 T7A06 T7A11">

An **oscillator** generates a signal at one specific frequency. A **mixer** combines two frequencies to shift a signal to another one.

<Mixer />

The **VFO** (variable frequency oscillator) is the tuning knob: it sets the **receive and transmit frequency**.

A **transverter** puts a mixer around your radio so it works on **another band**.

<Transverter />

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Oscillator | generates a frequency |
| Mixer | converts a signal from one frequency to another |
| VFO | sets the receive and transmit frequency |
| Transverter | converts a radio's input and output to another band |

</Concept>

<Concept id="modulation" title="Modulation puts your voice on a carrier" qs="T7A08">

Voice is too low in frequency to travel as a radio wave. **Modulation** combines speech with an **RF carrier**: AM varies its height, FM varies its frequency.

<ModulationWaves />

<Key>Speech + carrier = modulation.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="sens-sel" title="Sensitivity hears the faint. Selectivity separates the close." qs="T7A01 T7A04">

<SensSel />

| Term | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Sensitivity** | Can it **detect** a weak signal? |
| **Selectivity** | Can it **discriminate** between signals on nearby frequencies? |

<Mnemonic>Sensitivity = senses a faint signal. Selectivity = selects one signal from the crowd.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
