<Concept id="power-supply" title="A power supply: transformer, rectifier, filter, regulator" qs="T6D01 T6D05 T6D06">

Each stage does one job on the way from wall power to clean DC.

<PowerSupply />

- **Transformer** changes 120 V AC to a lower AC voltage.
- **Rectifier** (diodes) turns AC into varying DC.
- **Regulator** holds the output voltage steady.

<Key>Rectifier: AC to DC. Regulator: controls the voltage. Transformer: changes the AC voltage.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="relay" title="A relay is a switch worked by electricity" qs="T6D02">

A small current through a **coil** makes an electromagnet. It pulls a switch closed in a **separate circuit**, so a weak signal can control a big load.

<Relay />

<Key>Relay = electrically-controlled switch.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="resonance" title="Inductor + capacitor = a resonant circuit" qs="T6D08 T6D11">

Connect an **inductor** and a **capacitor**, in series or in parallel, and energy swings between them at one natural frequency. Radios use this to **tune** to a station.

<Resonance />

<Key>Resonant (tuned) circuit = an inductor and a capacitor.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="shielding" title="Shielded wire keeps unwanted signals out and in" qs="T6D03">

A metal **shield** wrapped around a wire stops unwanted signals from **coupling** into it, or from its own signal leaking out to nearby circuits. It does not lower resistance or carry more current.

<Shield />

<Key>Shield = prevent coupling of unwanted signals to or from the wire.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="jobs" title="Know each part's one-line job" qs="T6D04 T6D07 T6D09 T6D10">

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| **Meter** | shows an electrical quantity as a **number** |
| **LED** | the common **visual indicator** (FETs and Zener diodes are not) |
| **Integrated circuit (IC)** | many transistors and parts in **one package** |
| **Transistor** | **controls the flow of current** (component 2 in figure T-1) |

<Mnemonic>IC = Integrated Circuit: a whole circuit integrated into one chip.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
