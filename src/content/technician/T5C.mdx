<Concept id="hertz" title="kHz and MHz: mind the capital letters" qs="T5C06 T5C07">

Radio signals are alternating currents, counted in thousands (**kilohertz**) or millions (**megahertz**) of cycles per second.

<HzSymbols />

<Key>Lowercase k, capital M, always capital H and lowercase z.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">A lowercase **m** means milli, so mHz is a thousandth of a hertz, not mega.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="impedance" title="Impedance: total opposition to AC, in ohms" qs="T5C05 T5C12">

Resistance opposes any current. Capacitors and inductors add **reactance**, which opposes only AC. **Impedance** is the combined opposition to AC, and like both of them it is measured in **ohms**.

<Impedance />

<Key>Impedance = opposition to AC current flow. Unit: ohm.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="power" title="Power is volts times amperes" qs="T5C08 T5C09 T5C10 T5C11">

In a DC circuit, **watts = volts × amperes**. Picture a rectangle: width is voltage, height is current, area is power.

<Formula label="DC power">P = E × I</Formula>

<PowerCalc />

| Find | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Power** P | P = E × I | 13.8 V × 10 A = **138 W** |
| **Power** P | P = E × I | 12 V × 2.5 A = **30 W** |
| **Current** I | I = P ÷ E | 120 W ÷ 12 V = **10 A** |

<Key>Multiply volts by amperes for watts. To find current, divide watts by volts.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">A squared current (I²) goes with resistance (I² × R), never with volts.</Callout>

</Concept>
