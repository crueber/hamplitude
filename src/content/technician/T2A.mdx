<Concept id="offset" title="A repeater transmits and listens on different frequencies" qs="T2A01 T2A03 T2A07">

A repeater can't hear and talk on the same frequency, so its input and output are separated by a fixed **offset**. The offset can be plus or minus.

<Offset />

| Band | Common offset |
|---|---|
| **2 meters** | **600 kHz** |
| **70 centimeters** | **5 MHz** |

<Key>2 m: 600 kHz. 70 cm: 5 MHz. Offset is the gap between repeater transmit and receive.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="simplex" title="Simplex: same frequency, no repeater" qs="T2A11 T2A02 T2A10">

<Simplex />

**Simplex** means transmitting and receiving on the **same frequency**, directly station to station. The 2 m national FM simplex calling frequency is **146.520 MHz**.

A **band plan** is a **voluntary guideline** for which modes or activities go where within a band. It sits on top of the FCC's rules and privileges.

<Key>146.520 MHz: the 2 m FM simplex calling frequency. Band plans are voluntary.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="calling" title="Calling: their call sign first, then yours" qs="T2A04 T2A05 T2A06 T2A08 T2A09">

<Calling />

**CQ** means "calling any station". To answer one, say **their** call, then **yours**. To find a contact off-repeater, call CQ a few times with "this is" and your call, then pause and listen.

<Mnemonic>Reply: them, then me. On a repeater, "K2XYZ listening" says you're open for a call.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
