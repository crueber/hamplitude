<Concept id="doppler" title="Approaching raises the frequency. Receding lowers it." qs="T8B07 T8B09">

**Doppler shift** is a change in the frequency you receive, caused by **relative motion** between the satellite and you. Like a siren: higher pitch coming, lower going.

<DopplerPass />

The shift is larger on higher frequencies. It is zero only at closest approach.

<Callout kind="note" title="Spin fading">A satellite that rotates swings its antennas around, so your signal rises and falls. That is **spin fading**, caused by rotation. It is not Doppler.</Callout>

<Key>Doppler = motion changes frequency. Spin fading = rotation changes strength.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="tracking" title="Orbit numbers in, a pass plan out" qs="T8B10 T8B03 T8B06">

**LEO** is Low Earth Orbit: close to Earth, about **100 minutes** per lap. That is why a pass lasts only minutes.

<Tracking />

The input is the **Keplerian elements**: numbers that describe the orbit. A tracking program gives all three outputs: a live map, pass times with azimuth and elevation, and the Doppler-shifted frequency.

<Key>Keplerian elements describe the orbit. Not the satellite's power, not Doppler history.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="links" title="Uplink up on one band, downlink back on another" qs="T8B04 T8B08 T8B02 T8B12">

The **uplink** is you to the satellite. The **downlink** is satellite to you. **U/V** reads uplink first: U (70 cm) up, V (2 m) down.

<UVMode />

Satellites use **SSB, FM, and CW/data**, so all of these are correct. On a linear transponder, shout too loud and you **block other users**. Aim your downlink to match the beacon.

<UplinkLevel />

<Key>Use only enough power to match the beacon. More just crowds out everyone else.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="beacon" title="A beacon broadcasts satellite health to anyone" qs="T8B01 T8B05 T8B11">

A **beacon** is a transmission from the satellite carrying **telemetry**: the satellite's health and status (battery, temperature and so on). Anyone can receive it. Amateur satellite telemetry is not encrypted.

<Beacon />

<Key>Beacon = status broadcast. Telemetry = health and status. Anyone may listen.</Key>

</Concept>
