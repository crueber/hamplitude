<Concept id="bands" title="Know where 6 m and 2 m sit" qs="T1B03 T1B04 T1B02">

<T1B_BandMap />

Band names are roughly **300 ÷ frequency**, so 146.52 MHz is about 2 m. Learn the edges: **6 m is 50 to 54 MHz**, **2 m is 144 to 148 MHz**. 49.00 MHz is just below 6 m and in no amateur band.

Any Technician or higher can contact the **ISS** on VHF. No General license, no NASA approval.

<Mnemonic>6 m: 50 to 54. 2 m: 144 to 148.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="ten" title="On HF, voice is 10 m only: 28.300 to 28.500" qs="T1B01 T1B06">

<T1B_TenMeter />

Technician voice privileges on HF are **only on 10 m**, from 28.300 to 28.500 MHz. Below 28.300 is CW and digital.

<Key>HF voice: 10 m only, 28.300 to 28.500 MHz.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="modes" title="Voice and digital are everywhere above 50 MHz, except the CW slice" qs="T1B05 T1B07 T1B10">

<T1B_CwOnly />

**Digital modes** such as FT8 are allowed on 10, 6 and 2 m. **SSB** is allowed in at least part of every band above 50 MHz. The only CW-only slices are the bottom 100 kHz of 6 m and 2 m.

<Mnemonic>CW only: 50.0 to 50.1 and 144.0 to 144.1.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="edges" title="Share the band, and stay off the edge" qs="T1B08 T1B09">

<T1B_Secondary />

<T1B_BandEdge />

A signal is not a thin line. Sidebands, display error and drift can push it past a limit, so set your frequency **a little inside** the edge.

<Key>Secondary: yield. Band edge: leave margin.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="power" title="Power limits: 200 W on HF, 1500 W above 30 MHz" qs="T1B11 T1B12">
