<Concept id="squelch" title="Squelch mutes what is weaker than its threshold" qs="T4B03">

**Squelch** silences the speaker when no signal is present. Set higher, it also mutes weak signals. To hear a weak FM signal, lower the threshold until the **audio is on all the time**.

<Squelch />

<Key>Weak signal? Open the squelch so audio is always on.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="filters" title="Match filter bandwidth to the mode" qs="T4B08 T4B10">

A narrower filter blocks noise and nearby interference, but too narrow cuts the signal itself. Choose the bandwidth that **matches the mode**. An SSB voice signal fits best in **2400 Hz**.

<Filter />

<Key>Filter width matches the mode: SSB voice, 2400 Hz.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="tuning" title="Tuning: keypad, VFO, scan and RIT" qs="T4B02 T4B05 T4B04 T4B06">

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Keypad** or **VFO knob** | Enters the operating frequency |
| **Scan** | Tunes through a range of frequencies to check for activity |
| **RIT / Clarifier** | Shifts receive frequency only, to fix voice pitch on SSB |

CTCSS and DTMF are tones, not ways to enter a frequency.

<Rit />

Off-frequency **FM** sounds **distorted**. Off-frequency **SSB** sounds **too high or too low**, and RIT corrects it.

<Key>SSB pitch wrong? RIT or Clarifier. Off-frequency FM? Distorted.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="mic-gain" title="Too much microphone gain distorts" qs="T4B01">

<MicGain />

Keep the peaks inside the limit. Excess gain gives **distorted transmitted audio**.

<Key>Excessive mic gain means distorted audio.</Key>

</Concept>
