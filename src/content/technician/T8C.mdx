<Concept id="rdf" title="Point a directional antenna, then cross the bearings" qs="T8C01 T8C02">

**Radio direction finding (RDF)** finds where a signal comes from: noise sources, jammers, or a hidden transmitter in a fox hunt. A **directional antenna** hears best when it points at the source.

<Bearings />

<Key>Hidden transmitter hunt, interference, jamming: direction finding with a directional antenna.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="contest" title="Contests: many contacts, shortest exchange" qs="T8C03 T8C04">

A **contest** is an on-air competition to contact as many stations as possible in a set time. Be quick and clean.

| Good practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Send **only** your call and the required exchange | Saves time for everyone |
| Give your full call sign | Short or partial calls cause mistakes |
| Work each station once | Duplicates waste time |

<Key>Minimum information: your call sign and the contest exchange.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="grid" title="A grid locator is a letter-number code for a place" qs="T8C05">

A **grid locator** (Maidenhead grid square) names a geographic area with letters and numbers. Each extra pair of characters narrows it down.

<GridLocator />

You exchange grid squares in contests and on weak-signal modes. They mark **where**, not a direction or an elevation.

<Key>Letter, letter, number, number (then letters): a place on the map.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="internet" title="Gateways link radio to the internet" qs="T8C06 T8C07 T8C08 T8C09 T8C10 T8C11">

**VoIP** is voice carried over the internet as digital data. A **gateway** is a station that connects other amateur stations to the internet.

<NetLink />

| System | How you use it |
|---|---|
| **IRLP** | Links repeaters over the internet. From a radio, you control a node with **DTMF** tones |
| **EchoLink** | Needs no radio to start: use a computer or phone. **Register your call sign** and give **proof of license** first |

<Mnemonic>IRLP: radio and DTMF keypad tones. EchoLink: log in, no radio needed.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
