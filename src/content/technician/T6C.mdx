<Concept id="schematic" title="A schematic shows connections, nothing else" qs="T6C01 T6C12">

A **schematic** is a circuit diagram drawn with standard symbols. It shows **what connects to what**. It does not show wire length, size or what the parts look like.

<SchematicVsPicture />

<Key>Schematic = standard symbols + connections. Nothing about looks or lengths.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="basic-symbols" title="Basic parts: each symbol is a tiny picture of the part" qs="T6C02 T6C04 T6C05 T6C06">

Match the symbol to its picture. The long-and-short lines of a battery are stacked plates, a resistor zigzags because it resists, a capacitor is two plates with a gap.

<SymbolGallery group="basic" />

<Callout kind="tip" title="Exam figure T-2">The capacitor there has one flat and one curved plate, which usually means a polarized (electrolytic) type. It is still a **capacitor**.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="semi-symbols" title="Semiconductors: an arrow shows the way current flows" qs="T6C03 T6C07">

Diodes and LEDs have a triangle pointing the way conventional current flows. An **LED** adds light arrows leaving. A **transistor** is three leads meeting a thick bar, usually inside a circle, and has an arrow on the emitter.

<SymbolGallery group="semi" />

<Mnemonic>Triangle with a bar = diode. Add little arrows shining out = LED. Three leads in a circle = transistor.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="variable-symbols" title="An arrow on a part means it is adjustable" qs="T6C08 T6C10">

An arrow through or touching a part makes it **variable**: a resistor, capacitor or inductor you can adjust.

<SymbolGallery group="variable" />

<Key>Arrow = adjustable. Zigzag + arrow = variable resistor. Coil + arrow = variable inductor. Plates + arrow = variable capacitor.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="coil-symbols" title="Coils, transformers and antennas" qs="T6C09 T6C11">

Loops mean a coil. **Two coils side by side** with bars between them is a **transformer**. A flat-topped triangle on a stem, with a line down its middle, is an **antenna**.

<SymbolGallery group="coils" />

<Key>One coil = inductor. Two coupled coils = transformer. Triangle on a stem = antenna.</Key>

</Concept>
