<Concept id="broadcast" title="No broadcasting: the audience is the test" qs="T1D10 T1D02 T1D09 T1D07">

<T1D_Broadcast />

**Broadcasting** is transmission intended for the **general public**. It is prohibited. Some one-way transmissions are not broadcasting: event announcements, code practice, telecommand and telemetry. News gathering and broadcast support is allowed only when tied to the **immediate safety of life or protection of property**, and nothing else is available.

An **auxiliary station** is a one-way link between parts of a repeater system, such as a remote receiver feeding the main transmitter.

</Concept>
