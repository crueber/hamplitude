<Concept id="dummy-load" title="A dummy load lets you transmit without transmitting" qs="T7C01 T7C03">

<DummyLoad />

A **dummy load** is a **50 Ω non-inductive resistor on a heat sink**. It soaks up your power as heat, so test signals **don't go out over the air**.

<Key>Dummy load = test without radiating.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="swr" title="SWR tells you how well the antenna matches the line" qs="T7C04 T7C06 T7C08 T7C02">

When the antenna doesn't match the feed line, part of the power **reflects** back down the line. **SWR** (standing wave ratio) measures how much.

<SwrWaves />

| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **1:1** | perfect match, nothing reflected |
| 4:1 | **mismatch** |

| Instrument | Tells you |
|---|---|
| **Directional wattmeter** | forward and reflected power, so the SWR |
| **Antenna analyzer** | whether the antenna is **resonant** at your operating frequency |

<Key>1:1 is perfect. The higher the ratio, the worse the mismatch.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="loss" title="Lost power becomes heat" qs="T7C05 T7C07 T7C11">

Power lost in a feed line is **converted into heat**. It isn't radiated and it doesn't distort the signal.

<LineLoss />

**Foam dielectric** coax has **less loss per foot** than solid.

Solid-state transmitters **cut their output as SWR rises** to **protect the output transistors**.

<Key>Loss = heat. High SWR makes the radio back off to protect itself.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="coax-failure" title="Water is what kills coax" qs="T7C09 T7C10">

<CoaxLayers />

**Moisture contamination** causes coax to fail. That's why the outer jacket must resist **UV light**: sun damage cracks the jacket and **lets water in**.

<Callout kind="note" title="Not causes">Solder flux, fast power changes and running at 100% duty cycle don't kill coax.</Callout>

</Concept>
