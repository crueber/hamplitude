<Concept id="inspection" title="The FCC can inspect, any time" qs="T1F01">

Your station **and its records** must be available to an FCC representative **whenever they ask**. No advance notice, no warrant required.

<Key>Upon request, at any time.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="id" title="Identify: every 10 minutes and at the end" qs="T1F02 T1F03 T1F04 T1F05 T1F06">

<IdTimeline />

| Rule | Answer |
|---|---|
| **How often** | At least every **10 minutes** during a contact, and **at the end** |
| **Which call sign** | Your FCC-assigned call, even if you're using a tactical name like "Race Headquarters" |
| **Language** (phone) | **English** |
| **Method** (phone) | Say it on phone, **or** send it in CW |
| **Indicators** | "slash", "stroke" and "slant" are all fine: KL7CC/W3 |

<Mnemonic>10 minutes, and when you're done. Not "once per transmission", not 15.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="third-party" title="Third-party communication" qs="T1F07 T1F08">

<ThirdParty />

**Third-party communication** is a message from a control operator to another station's control operator **on behalf of someone else**.

When that someone isn't licensed and the other station is **foreign**, the other country must have a **third-party agreement** with the US.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Not required">The unlicensed person doesn't need to be a US citizen.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="repeaters" title="Repeaters: who is responsible?" qs="T1F09 T1F10">

<RepeaterFlow />

A **repeater station** automatically retransmits another station's signal on a different channel.

If a repeater relays something that breaks the rules by accident, the person who sent it is responsible: **the control operator of the originating station**. Not the repeater owner.

<Key>You are responsible for what *you* put through a repeater.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="clubs" title="Club station licenses" qs="T1F11">

A club station license needs a **trustee** and **at least four members**. The trustee doesn't have to be an Extra, and the club doesn't have to belong to the ARRL.

<Key>Four members minimum.</Key>

</Concept>
