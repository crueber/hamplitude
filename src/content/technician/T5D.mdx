<Concept id="ohm" title="Ohm's law: three letters, one relationship" qs="T5D01 T5D02 T5D03">

Voltage (**E**) pushes. Resistance (**R**) resists. The result is current (**I**) flowing.

<Formula label="Ohm's law">E = I × R</Formula>

<OhmsLaw />

Rearranged, it gives the other two:

| Find | Formula | In words |
|---|---|---|
| **Voltage** E | E = I × R | current times resistance |
| **Current** I | I = E ÷ R | voltage over resistance |
| **Resistance** R | R = E ÷ I | voltage over current |

<Key>Voltage is on top. To find anything else, divide E by the other one.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">Choices with a squared term (E², I²) are for **power**, not Ohm's law.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="solve" title="Solving one: pick the formula, plug in, check" qs="T5D04 T5D05 T5D06 T5D07 T5D08 T5D09 T5D10 T5D11 T5D12">

Every calculation question is the same three steps.

1. Write down what you know, and what you want.
2. Pick the formula for the unknown.
3. Plug in and sanity-check.

| Question | Formula | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 90 V, 3 A → **R**? | R = E ÷ I = 90 ÷ 3 | **30 Ω** |
| 120 V, 80 Ω → **I**? | I = E ÷ R = 120 ÷ 80 | **1.5 A** |
| 0.5 A through 2 Ω → **E**? | E = I × R = 0.5 × 2 | **1 V** |

<Mnemonic>Sanity check: more resistance means *less* current. If your current came out huge (9,600 A), you multiplied when you should have divided.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="series-parallel" title="Series shares current. Parallel shares voltage." qs="T5D13 T5D14">

<SeriesParallel />

- **Series**: one path. Every part carries the **same current**.
- **Parallel**: branches. Every part sees the **same voltage**.

<Mnemonic>Parallel = Potential (voltage) is the same. Series = Stream (current) is the same.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
