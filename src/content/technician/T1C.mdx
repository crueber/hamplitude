<Concept id="classes" title="Three classes are issued; anyone can go vanity" qs="T1C01 T1C02">

<T1C_Classes />

The FCC issues **Technician, General and Amateur Extra**. A **vanity call sign** is a call you choose yourself: **any licensed amateur** may request one.

<Key>Technician, General, Extra. Vanity: any licensee.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="call-format" title="Count letters to spot a call sign group" qs="T1C05">

<T1C_CallSign />

**Group D** is two letters, a number, then three letters.

</Concept>

<Concept id="term" title="Licenses: transmit when granted, 10 years, then grace" qs="T1C10 T1C08 T1C07 T1C09 T1C11">

<T1C_Lifecycle />

You may transmit once your grant shows in the FCC database, not when the paper or CSCE arrives. In the grace period you can renew, but you stay off the air until it is renewed.

<Mnemonic>10 years. Renew from 90 days out. 2 years grace, silent.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>

<Concept id="address" title="Keep your email and address current" qs="T1C04">

The FCC contacts you by **email**. If it can't reach you, it can **revoke your station license or suspend your operator license**.

<Key>Update your contact info in the FCC database.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="international" title="International and maritime operating" qs="T1C03 T1C06">

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What may you say** to other countries? | Amateur-radio topics and **personal remarks**. No business. |
| **Operating at sea** in international waters? | Allowed from a **US-documented vessel** with the **master's permission**. No special FCC authorization. |

<Key>Amateur and personal only. Vessel needs the master's okay.</Key>

</Concept>
