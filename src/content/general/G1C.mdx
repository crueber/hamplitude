<Concept id="power" title="1500 W PEP, except 200 W on 30 m" qs="G1C01 G1C02 G1C05 G1C06 G1C11">

<G1C_PowerLimits />

**30 m** (10.140 MHz) is the exception at **200 W PEP**. On 160, 12 and 10 m the limit is **1500 W PEP**.

<G1C_PepPoint />

<Key>Limit is PEP output from the transmitter.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="sixty" title="60 m: 2.8 kHz wide, and record your antenna gain" qs="G1C03 G1C04">

<G1C_Bandwidth60 />

On 60 m, a USB signal may be no more than **2.8 kHz** wide. No contact log or equipment record is required, but a non-dipole antenna's **gain must be recorded**.

</Concept>

<Concept id="protocol" title="New digital protocol: publish how it works" qs="G1C07">

<G1C_Paperwork />

The technical characteristics must be **publicly documented** so others can decode it. No FCC certificate or special license is needed.

</Concept>
