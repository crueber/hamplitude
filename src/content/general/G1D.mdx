<Concept id="ve" title="Volunteer Examiners: VEC-accredited, 18+, General or above" qs="G1D02 G1D04 G1D07 G1D08 G1D10">

<G1D_VeChecklist />

A **Volunteer Examiner Coordinator (VEC)** accredits VEs, not the FCC. A Technician exam needs **three VEs**, each General or higher.

</Concept>

<Concept id="csce" title="A CSCE lasts 365 days and unlocks General now" qs="G1D03 G1D06 G1D09">

<G1D_Csce />

A **CSCE** is your proof of passing. With it you may use any General or Technician segment right away. Until the FCC database shows the upgrade, identify with **"AG"** after your call sign when using General privileges.

<Key>365 days. "AG" until the database catches up.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="expired" title="Expired licenses: credit if you held General or above" qs="G1D01 G1D11">

<G1D_Expired />

After the **two-year grace period** you can't just renew. Prove the expired grant and **pass Element 2** again.

</Concept>

<Concept id="remote" title="Remote control: the station's country sets the rules" qs="G1D05 G1D12">

<G1D_Remote />

Running a **US station** from abroad needs your **US license** only. Running a **foreign station** from the US means that **country's rules** only.

</Concept>
