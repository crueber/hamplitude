<Concept id="fusing" title="Fuse only the hot wires, and size the breaker to the wire" qs="G0B01 G0B02 G0B03 G0B06">

In a four-conductor 240 V circuit (two hots, neutral, ground), fuses or breakers go **only in the hot wires**.

<Wires240 />

The breaker must protect the **thinnest wire**. **AWG 14** goes with a **15 A** breaker, **AWG 12** with **20 A**. A lower AWG number is thicker wire. The **National Electrical Code** covers the electrical safety of the station.

<Gauge />

<Key>Hots only. 20 A needs AWG 12, 15 A needs AWG 14.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="protectors" title="GFCIs and interlocks cut the power when something goes wrong" qs="G0B05 G0B12">

A **GFCI** trips when current flows from a **hot wire directly to ground**, for example through you. Current from hot to neutral through a load is normal.

<Gfci />

A **power supply interlock** removes **dangerous voltages when the cabinet is opened**. It does not sense heat or overvoltage.

<Interlock />

<Key>GFCI: leak to ground. Interlock: cabinet opened.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="lightning" title="Ground outside, arrest at the entry, bond everything" qs="G0B04 G0B11 G0B13">

<Lightning />

Put the lightning protection ground system **outside the building**. Mount **arrestors where the feed lines enter**. **Bond every ground rod together** with all other grounds, so no point rises above the rest during a strike.

<Key>Outside, at the entry, all bonded.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="tower" title="Before a climb: lock out the power, check the harness" qs="G0B07 G0B08">

<Climb />

Confirm the harness is **rated for the climber's weight** and **within its allowable service life**. If the tower supports electrically powered devices, **lock out and tag** every circuit that feeds it first. Do not unground the tower.

<Key>Lockout and tag, then a rated, in-date harness.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="hazards" title="Fumes and lead: ventilate the generator, wash after soldering" qs="G0B09 G0B10">

<Hazards />

Run an emergency generator in a **well-ventilated area**. Do not store fuel next to it. The danger from **lead-tin solder** is **lead contaminating food** if you do not wash your hands.

<Key>Generator: fresh air. Solder: clean hands.</Key>

</Concept>
