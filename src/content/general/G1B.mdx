<Concept id="antennas" title="Antennas: 200 ft is the federal line, PRB-1 limits local rules" qs="G1B01 G1B06">

<G1B_AntennaHeight />

Above **200 ft** (away from a public use airport) you must notify the FAA and register the structure with the FCC.

<G1B_Prb1 />

Cities and states may regulate antennas, but must **reasonably accommodate** amateur radio and use the **minimum practical** regulation.

</Concept>

<Concept id="beacons" title="Beacons: one-way propagation probes" qs="G1B02 G1B03 G1B09 G1B10">

<G1B_BeaconRules />

A beacon transmits to **observe propagation and reception**. It needs no coordination or publication. Just stay within the limits above.

<Key>100 W, one per band per site. Automatic HF beacons: 28.20 to 28.30.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="oneway" title="What a station may send and relay" qs="G1B04 G1B05">

<G1B_OneWay />

Amateur stations may broadcast one-way only in narrow cases such as **code practice**. Relaying government weather and propagation info is fine if **occasional**.

</Concept>

<Concept id="practice" title="Practice, abbreviations and foreign contacts" qs="G1B07 G1B08 G1B11">

<G1B_Practice />

<Key>The FCC sets good practice. Abbreviations: fine if clear. Foreign hams: yes, unless the ITU was told no.</Key>

</Concept>
