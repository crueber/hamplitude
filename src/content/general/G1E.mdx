<Concept id="third-party" title="Third-party traffic: license status and content decide" qs="G1E01 G1E05 G1E12">

<G1E_ThirdParty />

A revoked license disqualifies a third party. Citizenship and language don't. Remote control changes nothing: **if the rules allow third-party messages, they may go by remote control**.

</Concept>

<Concept id="repeater" title="A 10 m repeater needs a General control operator" qs="G1E02">

<G1E_Repeater10m />

The repeater is transmitting on 10 m, so **its control operator** needs HF privileges: at least **General**. The Technician's own license only covers the 2 m input.

</Concept>

<Concept id="care" title="Three situations that demand extra care" qs="G1E04 G1E07 G1E08">

<G1E_Care />

<Key>Near a monitoring station, on a secondary band, or with spread spectrum: avoid interference.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="regions" title="The Americas are ITU Region 2" qs="G1E06">

<G1E_Regions />

The ITU splits the world into three regions with their own allocations. **North and South America are Region 2.**

</Concept>

<Concept id="auto" title="Automatic digital stations and beacon frequencies" qs="G1E03 G1E11 G1E10">

<G1E_AutoDigital />

Automatic digital stations may talk to each other on **6 m and shorter wavelengths** and in **limited HF segments**. Elsewhere a human-controlled station must start the contact.
