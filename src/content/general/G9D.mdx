<Concept id="nvis" title="NVIS wants a low horizontal dipole" qs="G9D01">

**NVIS** (near vertical incidence skywave) sends signal almost **straight up** so it drops back **close by**, for short-skip links such as 40 m in daytime. A **horizontal dipole 1/10 to 1/4 λ** above ground radiates strongly overhead. At ½ λ high it has a null straight up, and a vertical radiates little overhead.

<G9D_Nvis />

<Key>Low horizontal dipole, 1/10 to 1/4 λ up, for NVIS.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="wire" title="End-fed half wave: very high impedance. One support: inverted V" qs="G9D02 G9D12">

Feed a half-wave wire at its **end** and you are at the **voltage maximum**: the feed point impedance is **very high**. Slide the feed point all the way out to see it.

<G9B_FeedPoint start={1} />

A dipole hung from **one central support**, ends sloping down, is an **inverted V**.

<G9D_InvertedV />

<Key>End-fed half wave: very high Z. One center support: inverted V.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="multiband" title="Traps and loading coils make one antenna fit many bands" qs="G9D04 G9D08 G9D11">

**Antenna traps** are parallel LC circuits that **enable multiband operation**: they block the higher band's signal from the outer wire, and act as loading coils on the lower band.

<G9D_Trap />

A **screwdriver** mobile antenna has a motor that changes the **base loading inductance** to retune.

<G9D_Screwdriver />

The catch: **multiband antennas have poor harmonic rejection**.

<Key>Traps: multiband. Screwdriver: motor-tuned base coil. Cost: harmonics.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="beams" title="Stacking narrows the beam up and down; log-periodics cover a wide band" qs="G9D05 G9D06 G9D07">

**Stacking** horizontally polarized Yagis **narrows the main lobe in elevation**, not in azimuth.

<G9C_Stack />

A **log-periodic** has elements whose **length and spacing vary logarithmically** along the boom. Its advantage is **wide bandwidth**, not extra gain per element.

<G9D_LogPeriodic />

<Key>Stack: narrower in elevation. Log-periodic: wide bandwidth.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="loops" title="Loops have nulls broadside; Beverages are for receiving" qs="G9D03 G9D09 G9D10">

An **electrically small loop** (under 1/10 λ around) has **nulls broadside** to the loop and its strongest signal in the plane of the loop. A **halo** is a loop lying flat: it is **omnidirectional in its own plane**.

<G9D_LoopPattern />

A **Beverage** is a long low wire for **directional receiving** on MF and low HF.

<G9D_Beverage />

<Key>Small loop: null broadside. Halo: omni in its plane. Beverage: directional receiving.</Key>

</Concept>
