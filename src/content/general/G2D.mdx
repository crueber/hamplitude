<Concept id="vm" title="Volunteer Monitors help amateurs police themselves" qs="G2D01 G2D02 G2D03">

The **Volunteer Monitor Program** enlists amateurs to **monitor the airwaves for rules violations**. Its aim is **self-regulation**: encourage operators to follow the rules. (Not exams, not repeater coordination, not emergency comms.)

<G2D_Triangulate />

To find a stuck carrier on a repeater input, monitors **compare beam headings** from their own homes. Where the headings cross is the source.

<Key>VM: volunteers watching for violations. Find a carrier by crossing beam headings.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="azimuthal" title="Long path points 180° from short path" qs="G2D04 G2D06">

An **azimuthal projection map** is centred on **your** station and shows **true bearings and distances** from it.

<G2D_Azimuthal />

For a **long-path** contact, point the beam **180° from the short-path heading**.

<Key>Long path = short-path heading plus or minus 180°.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="cq" title="CQ, who you are, then listen" qs="G2D05 G2D07 G2D11">

<G2D_CqCall />

Spell call signs with the **NATO phonetic alphabet**: Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta... (Able Baker, Adam Boy and America Boston are other alphabets.) Reports go first so each station can **operate according to conditions**.

<Key>"CQ CQ CQ, this is" call sign, pause. Phonetics: Alpha, Bravo, Charlie.</Key>

</Concept>
