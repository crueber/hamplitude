<Concept id="no-priority" title="Nobody owns a frequency, so ask first and share" qs="G2B01 G2B06 G2B03">

Except in **emergencies**, **no station has priority** on a frequency. Not nets, not QSOs in progress, not contests. So before calling CQ, ask: **"QRL?" plus your call** on CW, or **"is the frequency in use?" plus your call** on phone.

<G2B_AskFirst />

<Key>No priority. Ask "QRL?", and settle interference by agreement.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="spacing" title="Space stations by their own width" qs="G2B04 G2B05">

<G2B_Spacing />

A CW signal is very narrow, so **150 to 500 Hz** apart is enough. SSB is about 3 kHz wide, so allow **2 to 3 kHz**.

<Key>CW: 150 to 500 Hz. SSB: 2 to 3 kHz.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="band-plan" title="Band plans are voluntary. Follow them." qs="G2B07 G2B08">

<G2B_DxWindow />

A **band plan** is not an FCC rule. Following it is **good amateur practice**. Example: on 6 m, **50.100 to 50.125 MHz** is for contacts with stations **outside the 48 contiguous states**.

<Key>Band plan = voluntary, but follow it. 50.1 to 50.125: DX only.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="distress" title="Distress call? Acknowledge it first" qs="G2B02 G2B10">

<G2B_Distress />

Your contact can wait. **Acknowledge** the station in distress and **find out what assistance is needed**. In your own nets, plan ahead with a **backup frequency**.

<Key>Hear distress: acknowledge, then ask what is needed.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="races" title="RACES: licensed operators, 1 hour of drills a week" qs="G2B09 G2B11">

<G2B_Races />

**RACES** is the emergency service for disaster relief. Its control operator must hold an **FCC-issued amateur operator license**. Routine **training drills and tests** are limited to **1 hour per week**.

<Key>RACES: amateur license, 1 hour a week of routine drills.</Key>

</Concept>
