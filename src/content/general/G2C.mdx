<Concept id="qsk" title="Full break-in: listen between your own dits" qs="G2C01">

With **full break-in (QSK)** the receiver comes alive in the gaps **between characters and elements**, so you can hear the other station interrupt you. Without it, you flip a send/receive switch before you can listen.

<G2C_Qsk />

<Key>QSK: receive between code elements.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="q-signals" title="Q signals: a statement, or a question with ?" qs="G2C02 G2C04 G2C09 G2C10 G2C11">

<G2C_QSignals />

<Mnemonic>QRL? asks "is this frequency in use?" Ask it before calling on a clear-sounding frequency.</Mnemonic>

Technician review: **QRM** is man-made interference, **QRN** natural noise (static), **QSY** change frequency.

</Concept>

<Concept id="prosigns" title="Prosigns: AR ends it, KN names who may reply" qs="G2C03 G2C08 G2C07">

<G2C_Prosigns />

**AR** closes a formal message. **KN** means *go ahead, only the named station*. In a signal report, a trailing **C** flags a **chirpy** signal.

<Key>AR = end of message. KN = only the named station. C = chirp.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="tuning" title="Answer at or below the CQ speed. Zero-beat the caller." qs="G2C05 G2C06">

Reply at **the fastest speed you can copy comfortably, but no faster than the CQ**. Then **zero beat**: tune your transmit frequency to **match the received signal**, so they hear you right where they listen.

<G2C_ZeroBeat />

<Key>Not faster than the CQ. Zero beat = same frequency.</Key>

</Concept>
