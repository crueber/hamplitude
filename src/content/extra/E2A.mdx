<Concept id="orbit" title="The orbit sets the lap time and the pass direction" qs="E2A01 E2A06 E2A10">

**Keplerian elements** are the parameters that define an orbit (size, shape, tilt, timing). Feed them to tracking software and it predicts every pass.

<E2A_GroundTrack />

A pass heading from south to north is **ascending**. Bigger orbit, longer lap. At one special height a lap takes exactly one Earth turn, so a **geostationary** satellite (over the equator) hangs over one spot. A LEO satellite sweeps past in minutes.

<E2A_Orbit />

<Key>Ascending = south to north. Geostationary = fixed in the sky. LEO, HEO and the rest all move.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="transponder" title="A linear transponder is a flipping mirror for a whole band" qs="E2A02 E2A03 E2A07 E2A08">

A **linear transponder** amplifies a whole passband without demodulating it, so **any mode** (SSB, CW, SSTV, PSK, packet, FM) passes through. An **inverting** one mixes the uplink with a local oscillator and sends the **difference**: the band is mirrored, so USB becomes LSB. Doppler on the uplink and downlink then push opposite ways, which reduces the net shift.

<E2A_Transponder />

The downlink power is shared. A strong uplink grabs more of it and **reduces downlink power for everyone else**, so keep your ERP low.

<Key>Inverting: band flips, sidebands swap, difference product is sent. Keep power low.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="modes" title="A mode name is two band letters: up, then down" qs="E2A04 E2A05 E2A09">

The **mode** of a satellite is its **uplink and downlink frequency bands**. Each letter is a range. **L band** is 23 cm and **S band** is 13 cm.

<E2A_ModeBands />

<Key>Mode = uplink band / downlink band. L = 23 cm, S = 13 cm.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="circular" title="Circular polarization shrugs off twisting waves" qs="E2A11">

A spinning satellite (**spin modulation**) and the ionosphere (**Faraday rotation**) twist the wave's polarization. A linear antenna fades as the twist passes through 90°. A **circularly polarized** antenna does not care.

<E2A_Polarization />

<Key>Twisting signal? Use circular polarization.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="store-forward" title="Store-and-forward is a mailbox in orbit" qs="E2A12 E2A13">

Digital satellites **hold messages on board for later download**. You upload on one pass; the satellite carries it and delivers on a later pass. The technique is called **store-and-forward**.

<E2A_StoreForward />

<Key>Store-and-forward = hold a message, deliver it later.</Key>

</Concept>
