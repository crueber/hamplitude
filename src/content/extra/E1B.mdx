<Concept id="spurious" title="Spurious: outside the bandwidth and avoidable" qs="E1B01 E1B02">

<E1B_Spurious />

A **spurious emission** is energy outside the signal's **necessary bandwidth** that you could cut without changing the message it carries. It is defined by that, not by an exact dB level.

On HF, digital voice and slow-scan TV stay within about **3 kHz**, roughly one SSB voice channel.

</Concept>

<Concept id="protect" title="Protect the parties your signal could hurt" qs="E1B03 E1B04 E1B05 E1B08">

<E1B_Protect />

Each rule says how much you must give way. A radiolocation complaint about your 70 cm repeater means the control operator must **stop or fix it**. Broadcast interference is handled by **time-of-day limits**, not a permanent ban.

</Concept>

<Concept id="antennas" title="Antennas: airport rules, then PRB-1" qs="E1B06 E1B07 E1B11">

<E1B_Zoning />

Two different layers. **PRB-1** is about **state and local zoning** and asks for **reasonable accommodation**. Airports are a separate, federal question.

<Key>PRB-1: reasonable accommodation. Not unlimited antennas.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="races" title="RACES: certified station, your own frequencies" qs="E1B09 E1B10">

<E1B_Races />

Any licensed amateur station certified by the **responsible civil defense organization** for its area can operate under RACES rules. It uses **the amateur frequencies its control operator is allowed**, not special channels.

</Concept>
