<Concept id="skin" title="Skin effect: at RF, current hugs the surface" qs="E5D01 E5D08">

As frequency rises, RF current is pushed into a thin layer at the conductor's surface. Less metal carries it, so **resistance rises with frequency**. It is also the main RF loss in film capacitors.

<E5D_SkinEffect />

<Key>Higher frequency: current nearer the surface, resistance higher.</Key>

<Callout kind="warn" title="Trap">Skin effect is about frequency, not temperature or electron mobility.</Callout>

</Concept>

<Concept id="parasitics" title="Every real part hides a bit of the opposite part" qs="E5D02 E5D05 E5D06 E5D07">

Leads and windings add small **parasitic** reactances: wire has inductance, and neighbouring turns act as a capacitor. The part's own (nominal) reactance and its parasitic reactance combine into a **self-resonance**. Beyond it the part behaves like its opposite.

<E5D_Parasitics />

| Part | Parasitic | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Inductor | **inter-turn capacitance** | creates its self-resonance |
| Electrolytic capacitor | **inductance** | unsuitable at RF |
| Any part at VHF and up | lead inductance | keep leads short to **minimise inductive reactance** |

<Key>Self-resonance = nominal reactance meeting parasitic reactance. Short leads keep parasitics small.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="length" title="At microwave frequencies even short wires are long" qs="E5D04 E5D10">

**Electrical length** is length measured in wavelengths. A signal shifts 360° across each wavelength, so the phase shift along a connection grows with its length and the frequency.

<E5D_ElectricalLength />

- Short connections at microwave frequencies **reduce phase shift** along the connection.
- A conductor's **diameter** also matters: a thicker conductor has a **greater** electrical length.

<Key>Phase shift = 360° × length ÷ wavelength. Higher frequency, shorter wavelength, bigger shift.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="power" title="Reactive power is stored and returned, never used up" qs="E5D03 E5D09 E5D11 E5D12">

An ideal inductor or capacitor stores energy in a field, then hands it back. Voltage and current are **90° out of phase**, so over a cycle the power averages **zero**. This wattless, nonproductive **reactive power** is not dissipated as heat. Only resistance consumes **real power**.

<E5D_PowerTriangle />

<Formula label="Real power">P = I² × R</Formula>

100 Ω resistor in series with 100 Ω XL at 1 A: P = 1² × 100 = **100 W**. Total is 141.4 VA, but only the resistor's 100 W is real.

<Key>Real power uses R only. Reactive power is wattless and nonproductive.</Key>

</Concept>
