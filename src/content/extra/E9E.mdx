| System | Signature |
|---|---|
| **Gamma** | shield to element center, center conductor tapped to one side, **series capacitor cancels inductive reactance**. Also shunt-feeds a **grounded tower** at its base |
| **Beta / hairpin** | element **insulated from the boom**, an **inductance across the feed point**. Needs a **capacitive** feed point (element shorter than ½ λ) |
| **Stub** | short piece of line **in parallel** with the feed line at the feed point |

<Key>Series C = gamma. Hairpin inductance needs a capacitive element.</Key>

<Concept id="qsection" title="A quarter-wave section transforms impedance, reflection coefficient rates the result" qs="E9E06 E9E07">

A **quarter-wave line** turns a load into **Zq² ÷ Z load**. To match 100 Ω to 50 Ω, you need Zq = √(100 × 50) = **70.7 Ω**: pick the closest available, **75 Ω**. What is left over is described by the **reflection coefficient** Γ = (Z load − Z₀) ÷ (Z load + Z₀), which sets the SWR.

<QSection />

<Key>Zq = √(Z1 × Z2). Γ describes how a load meets a line.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="wilkinson" title="A Wilkinson divider splits power and stays matched" qs="E9E08">

<Wilkinson />

A **Wilkinson divider** splits power **equally between two 50 Ω loads** while the input stays **50 Ω**. Each output sits behind a quarter-wave 70.7 Ω section that transforms its 50 Ω load up to 100 Ω, and two 100 Ω in parallel make 50 Ω. A resistor between the outputs keeps them isolated from each other.

<Key>Wilkinson: equal split, 50 Ω in, 50 Ω out.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="phasing" title="Phasing lines steer the pattern" qs="E9E11">

Drive two or more elements through **phasing lines** (extra line length = delay) and the signals add in some directions and cancel in others. That **controls the radiation pattern**: it doesn't change band coverage, harmonics or take-off angle by itself.

<Phasing />

<Key>Phasing lines set element phase. Phase sets the pattern.</Key>

</Concept>
