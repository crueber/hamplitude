<Concept id="common-mode" title="Common-mode current makes cables radiate. Choke it." qs="E4E04 E4E05 E4E07 E4E08">

<CommonMode />

**Common-mode current** flows **equally on all conductors** of an unshielded multiconductor cable. On shielded cable, common-mode currents on the **shield and conductors** are what make it radiate or receive interference.

| Source | Cure |
|---|---|
| Automobile charging system (alternator) | **ferrite chokes** on the charging system leads |
| Line-driven AC motor | **brute-force AC-line filter in series** with the motor's power leads |

<Key>Same direction on everything = antenna. Ferrite chokes block it.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="ground-panel" title="One ground panel, with the surge protector on it" qs="E4E13 E4E14">

<SinglePoint />

A **single point ground panel** ensures **all lightning protectors activate at the same time**: they share one ground, so no one cable is left at a different potential. Install the station **AC surge protector on that panel**.

<Key>One panel, one ground, every protector on it.</Key>

</Concept>
