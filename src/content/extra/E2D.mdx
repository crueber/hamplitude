<Concept id="path-modes" title="Pick the mode for the path: MSK144 meteors, Q65 Moon" qs="E2D01 E2D03">

**Meteor scatter** bounces signals off a short-lived ionized trail. **MSK144** is designed for it. **EME** (Earth-Moon-Earth, moonbounce) bounces them off the Moon. **Q65** is designed for it.

<E2D_WeakPaths />

<Key>Meteor scatter = MSK144. EME = Q65.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="weak-modes" title="JT65 digs a many-tone signal out of the noise" qs="E2D05 E2D09 E2D02">

**JT65** is a weak-signal mode: it **decodes signals at very low signal-to-noise ratio**. It uses **multitone AFSK**, one of many audio tones per symbol. Its sibling modes FT8 and FT4 swap the signal report for your **grid square** in a VHF contest exchange.

<E2D_Jt65 />

<Key>JT65: very low SNR, multitone AFSK. VHF contest FT8/FT4: grid square replaces SNR.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="eme-timing" title="EME contacts run on the clock, taking turns" qs="E2D06">

The echo from the Moon returns about 2.5 seconds later and is faint. So stations do not chase echoes. They agree on **time-synchronous transmissions that alternate** between the two stations.

<E2D_EmeSequence />

<Key>EME = clock-locked, alternating transmit and receive periods.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="aprs-frame" title="APRS beacons are AX.25 UI frames" qs="E2D04 E2D07 E2D08">

**APRS** (real-time tracking, such as balloons) uses the **AX.25** packet protocol. A beacon is an **Unnumbered Information (UI)** frame: just sent, with no connection and no acknowledgment.

<E2D_AprsFrame />

<Key>APRS = AX.25, beacon = UI frame. Tracks balloons.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="aprs-path" title="Digipeaters repeat APRS packets. WIDE3-1 = 1 hop left." qs="E2D10 E2D11">

APRS stations relay data with **packet digipeaters**. In **WIDE3-1**, the first number is the **hops requested** (three). The second counts the hops **remaining** (one), going down by one at each digipeater.

<E2D_AprsPath />

<Key>WIDEn-N: n hops requested, N hops remaining.</Key>

</Concept>
