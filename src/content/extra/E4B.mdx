<Concept id="meter-limits" title="A reading is only as good as the instrument's reference and loading" qs="E4B01 E4B02">

A frequency counter counts cycles over a timed gate, so its accuracy comes from its **time base** (the internal oscillator that sets the gate).

A voltmeter's **ohms-per-volt** rating gives its input impedance: **full-scale reading × ohms per volt**. A low value loads the circuit under test and the reading sags.

<OhmsPerVolt />

<Key>Counter: time base. Voltmeter: full scale × Ω/V = input impedance.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="s-params" title="S parameters: two digits name two ports" qs="E4B03 E4B04 E4B07">

<SParams />

The subscripts name the **port or ports where measurements are made**. **S21** (in at port 1, measured at port 2) is **forward gain**. **S11** (in and measured at port 1) is **input return loss**, the reflection coefficient (equivalent to VSWR).

<Key>S21 = forward gain. S11 = input reflection.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="vna" title="A vector network analyzer: calibrate with three loads, then measure a device's response" qs="E4B05 E4B09 E4B11">

<VnaCal />

Calibrate with a **short, an open and a 50 Ω load**.

| A VNA measures | A VNA does not measure |
|---|---|
| Filter frequency response | Phase noise |
| Input impedance | Pulse rise time |
| Output impedance | Forward power |
| Reflection coefficient | |

<Key>Short, open, 50 Ω. Then response, impedance and reflection.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="directional" title="The load absorbs forward minus reflected power" qs="E4B06">

<Directional />

<Formula label="Absorbed power">100 W forward − 25 W reflected = **75 W** absorbed</Formula>

<Key>What is reflected is not absorbed.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="q-bandwidth" title="Q comes from the bandwidth of the frequency response" qs="E4B08">

<QBandwidth />

Sweep a tuned circuit and read the width of its response between the half-power points. Narrow means **high Q**.

<Key>Q = centre frequency ÷ bandwidth.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="two-tone" title="Test SSB distortion with two audio tones and a spectrum analyzer" qs="E4B10">

<TwoTone />

Feed **two AF tones that are not harmonically related** into the transmitter. Intermodulation shows up as **extra signals on the RF output**, seen with a **spectrum analyzer**. A single tone cannot show intermodulation.

<Key>Two AF tones in, spectrum analyzer on the RF out.</Key>

</Concept>
