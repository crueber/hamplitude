<Concept id="structure" title="FCC, then VEC, then VE: who does what" qs="E1E01 E1E02 E1E03 E1E04">

<E1E_Roles />

A **VEC** is an organization with an **FCC agreement** to coordinate, prepare and administer exams. It **maintains the question pools**, and **confirms** that a VE applicant meets the FCC requirements. VEs are the volunteers who give the exam.

<Key>The VEC accredits and keeps the pools. The FCC doesn't.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="session" title="In the session: every VE is responsible" qs="E1E06 E1E07 E1E08 E1E09">

<E1E_Session />

Every administering VE is responsible for conduct and supervision, not just a session manager. Rule-breakers are stopped at once, and fraud costs the VE their licenses.

</Concept>

<Concept id="paperwork" title="After the exam: fail returns it, pass goes to the VEC" qs="E1E05 E1E10 E1E11">

<E1E_Paperwork />

A failed exam: **hand the application back** to the examinee. A pass: **three VEs certify**, then the team submits the paperwork **to the VEC**.

</Concept>
