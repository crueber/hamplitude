<Concept id="vf" title="Waves are slower in a line, so it is electrically longer than it looks" qs="E9F01 E9F02 E9F03 E9F06">

**Velocity factor** = wave speed in the line ÷ speed of light. The **insulating dielectric** sets it: air is about 1, plastic slows the wave. A slower wave has a shorter wavelength, so a physical length holds **more wavelengths**: electrical length exceeds physical length.

<Velocity />

<Key>Physical length = electrical length × VF. Dielectric sets VF.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="stubs" title="A shorted or open line looks like a short, an open, or a reactance" qs="E9F04 E9F09 E9F10 E9F11 E9F12">

What the generator sees depends on the **length**. Every **quarter wavelength** the picture flips (short becomes open); every **half wavelength** it repeats. In between: **under a quarter wave, a shorted line is inductive and an open line is capacitive**.

<StubReactance />

| Far end | ⅛ λ | ¼ λ | ½ λ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shorted** | inductive | very high Z | very low Z |
| **Open** | capacitive | very low Z | very high Z |

<Key>Shorted: ¼ λ flips it, ½ λ copies it. Open is the mirror image.</Key>

</Concept>

<Concept id="types" title="Open wire loses least; foam beats solid; microstrip lives on a board" qs="E9F05 E9F07 E9F08">

<LineTypes />

**Parallel-conductor (open-wire)** line has **lower loss** than plastic-dielectric coax. **Foam** coax versus **solid**, all else equal: **lower loss, higher velocity factor, lower safe maximum voltage**. All three.

<Key>More air in the dielectric: faster, lower loss, less voltage handling.</Key>

</Concept>
