<Concept id="roles" title="Telemetry comes down, telecommand goes up" qs="E1D01 E1D03 E1D10 E1D11">

<E1D_Roles />

**Telemetry:** one-way transmission of measurements from a distance. **Telecommand:** one-way transmission that initiates, modifies or terminates a device's functions. A **space telecommand station** does that to a **space station**.

**Who qualifies:** a telecommand station is any station **designated by the space station licensee**. An **Earth station** can be any amateur station, always within the control operator's license privileges.

</Concept>

<Concept id="special" title="Space links may go one-way and encrypted" qs="E1D12 E1D02">

<E1D_Special />

Space stations, beacons and telecommand stations may transmit **one-way**. Repeaters, forwarding stations and automatic digital stations may not. **Encryption** is allowed only for telecommand signals from a **space telecommand station**.

</Concept>

<Concept id="posted" title="Telecommand on or near Earth: post it, 1 W for models" qs="E1D04 E1D05 E1D06">

<E1D_Posted />

A balloon telemetry station still **identifies with its call sign**. Nothing else is required in the ID.

</Concept>

<Concept id="bands" title="Space stations: four HF bands, 2 m, 70 cm, 13 cm" qs="E1D07 E1D08 E1D09">

These are the bands the pool asks about, not every space allocation.

<Mnemonic>HF: 40, 20, 15, 10. VHF: 2 m only. UHF: 70 cm and 13 cm.</Mnemonic>

</Concept>
